Create the portal's account

The portal has no account yet. On the hub's machine, run:

~/.local/bin/ringback-hub portal-user NAME
  1. It asks for a password twice.
  2. It prints a setup key: add it to your authenticator app, such as Passwords on the iPhone.
  3. It asks for the passcode the app shows, and keeps the account once it matches.

This page then asks you to sign in.
